CarDoc Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

CarDoc is a vehicle diagnostics application that connects to compatible OBD-II adapters and helps users view diagnostic trouble codes, live vehicle data, vehicle information, and diagnostic reports.

Information CarDoc processes

CarDoc may process vehicle diagnostic information such as diagnostic trouble codes, sensor readings, VIN information, adapter connection details, and diagnostic history. This information is used to provide the app's diagnostic features.

Local storage

Diagnostic history, app preferences, connection preferences, and translation cache data may be stored locally on the user's device. CarDoc does not require a user account.

Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and USB

CarDoc can connect to compatible OBD-II adapters using Bluetooth Classic, Bluetooth Low Energy, Wi-Fi, or USB. Bluetooth permissions are used to discover and connect to nearby OBD-II adapters. On older Android versions, location permission may be required by Android for Bluetooth discovery. CarDoc does not use this permission to track or share the user's location.

Internet access and translation

CarDoc includes multilingual features. Internet access may be used to download language models required by Google ML Kit and, when needed, to request text translation from an external translation service. Text submitted for translation may be sent to that service solely to provide the requested translation.

Reports and sharing

CarDoc can create and share diagnostic reports and CSV files when the user chooses to do so. Sharing is initiated by the user through Android's system sharing interface.

Advertising and payments

The current version of CarDoc does not contain advertising and does not provide in-app purchases or paid digital features.

Data sale

CarDoc does not sell users' personal information.

Children

CarDoc is a general vehicle diagnostics utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when CarDoc features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact information shown on the CarDoc Google Play listing.
